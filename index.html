Built around the dirt

MotoHeim is a bike sim first. The ground, the weather and the rider all react to what you do.

Ruts that stay

Wheels stamp the ground, the surface darkens and displaces, and later laps ride in the grooves you cut. Ruts are saved per track.

Real suspension physics

Raycast wheels with spring, damper and bump stop, a tyre friction model, and a four-stroke engine with clutch slip, gearbox and engine braking.

A rider with weight

The rider is posed live. They lean into corners, brace for landings, fold on compressions and get thrown by a nose slap.

Weather and time of day

Fog, storms and a moving clock change grip, visibility and mood from one ride to the next.

Breakable trail furniture

Fences, branch piles and logs hold at low speed and splinter into physics pieces when you hit them fast.

Keyboard or gamepad

Xbox and PlayStation controllers work out of the box, with tunable steering sensitivity and dead zone.

One bike, plenty to do

Start in the forest, then take the same skills to a gate drop.

  • Free ride. Pick a trail, chase your best time and race your saved ghost.
  • Special tests. Short timed stages across the whole trail, scored against a leaderboard.
  • Time-card enduro. Long point-to-point events with time controls and timed tests, up to 9.7 km.
  • Poker Run. Collect cards at gates, swap on a detour, and cash in your hand for credits and prizes.
  • Motocross and supercross racing. Gate starts against a field of bots in their own kits, on eight outdoor layouts plus stadium tracks.
  • Championships. Chain a special test and races into a series, with points, results and unlocks.
  • Free roam. An open forest with discoveries, wildlife, hunting, fishing, a canoe, fast travel and a journal.
  • Your tracks. Load community tracks from other motocross games, or shape your own trail with the in-game spline tool.

Ride with friends

Racing already runs on player-hosted multiplayer over LAN or direct connection. Co-op free roam, where friends join your world to ride, fish and hunt together, is the next big step. No dedicated servers and no accounts.
